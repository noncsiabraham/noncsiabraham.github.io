Terra Kontra Festival 2025

Terra Kontra is an electronic music festival that takes place every year in Felsőtárkány, Hungary. Since its founding in 2022, the Terra Kontra collective has been represented on the domestic underground scene, with three festivals and countless club nights already under their belt. Terra is unique on the Hungarian scene not only because of the shared love of music behind the team, but also because of their (meta)communication with the community and their tendency towards constant renewal.

With Petri Csenge we have built two installations for this year's Terra Kontra x Pina Festival. The textile patterns we created were inspired by the Bükk region's topographical map, protected plant and animal species.

Subaquatica 2025 // MOME Filmstudio

The moment before falling asleep, swirling images and below. Diving in the deep, sounds and lights grow dimmer. A dreamlike arrival, you don't know how you got here. Waking up in a new place, you must scout the space. Subaquatica is an audiovisual event series that simultaneously engages both sight and hearing through the fusion and dialogue of sound and image. For its first edition, the acts will exclusively feature MOME students and alumni. Curators: Ármin Törkenczy, Máté Erős.

Collaboration with Csenge Petri in creating a motion picture set to music by Tibor Klöpfler-Topor.

Intermezzo Festival 2025 // House of Music Hungary

The visual work created for the Obroni collective for the Intermezzo Festival is based on the band's vibrant energy and diverse musical world. The goal was to create a visual experience that works in harmony with the music: light, movement, and form react to each other, following the rhythm of the sound to create a lively, pulsating atmosphere. The displayed visual was edited in real time during the performance, with the audience's reactions and the band's performance influencing the visuals.
